Privacy notice

How this website handles information when you browse or get in touch.

Updated September 21, 2026. This website presents Alvan Price’s work and consulting services through So Atlanta Operations LLC. Privacy questions can be sent to ap@soatlantastore.com.

Browsing this site

The site’s own code does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or embedded scheduling tools. Fonts and images are served with the site. The hosting service may process connection information, including IP addresses and request details, to deliver and secure the website.

Appearance preferences

If you choose light or night mode, the site saves that choice in your browser’s local storage under “ap-theme.” It is not sent to us. It remains until you clear it or your browser clears site data. You can remove it on the preferences page.

Contacting Alvan

Email links open your email application. Information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, and inquiry, is processed by the email services involved and used to respond and discuss the work. Please avoid sending sensitive information in an initial inquiry.

External websites

Links to services such as LinkedIn and GitHub take you to independently operated websites with their own privacy practices.

Questions and requests

Contact the address above about access, correction, or deletion of information you have provided. The rights that apply depend on your location and the circumstances. Retention and handling of client records are addressed separately for an engagement.
