Operational visibility at scale

From scattered information to a working picture of statewide operations.

A growing network of remote sites had no unified business intelligence for site health, assets, personnel, repairs, or recurring failures. I combined Jira workflows, InvenTree inventory, PagerDuty, Uptime Kuma, AI-assisted reporting, cloud services, and underused company servers into an operational layer the team could use.

  • Created visibility across approximately 1,260 devices at 21 sites and counting.
  • Enabled the team to identify and address problems before customers reported them.
  • Improved the evidence available for supplier, customer, and third-party audits.
  • Translated alerts and technical data into clearer reporting for executives.

Continuity and repair

Repair time stopped being the same thing as customer downtime.

The inherited system design lacked meaningful redundancy, business continuity, and disaster recovery. A repair involving an overseas team could leave one site waiting for weeks. I introduced redundancy, proposed out-of-band management, established better repair tracking, and helped move the workshop toward centralized system images and hot-swappable replacements.

  • Reduced comparable repair turnaround from weeks to approximately 72 hours.
  • Used hot-swapping to keep the customer from experiencing the workshop repair interval.
  • Turned repair history into evidence for broader system upgrades.
  • Reduced the need to send someone to a remote site simply to restore access.
